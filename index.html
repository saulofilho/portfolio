About

Security that works in Portuguese

Most AI security tools are designed in English. I focus on the Brazilian context: attacks written in Portuguese, detection of CPF, CNPJ and Pix data leaking in responses, and what Brazil's data protection law (LGPD) requires from anyone who ships a chatbot.

I have been writing software since 2017, across e-commerce, healthcare and the public sector. Today I work on data and security for RD Station Marketing at TOTVS. Before that I was a copywriter and digital strategist, which still shows in how I explain what I build.

I like tools that leave the person in control. My projects run locally, log every action and plug into CI to break the build when something gets worse.
