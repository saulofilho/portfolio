Portfolio / SF–1

Hi, I’m Saulo.

I write software that keeps AI honest, tools that poke at systems before someone else does, and firmware that makes a tiny groovebox play salsa.

I started out writing ads and drifted into writing code, which turned out to be the same job with stricter reviewers. These days I work on security for language models and web apps, mostly in the open and almost always with a dry-run switch.

If something here is useful to you, take it. If it’s broken, tell me. If you want to build something together, the door is at the bottom of the page.
